## 1. Getting Started

### Accessing the Platform

To access the Chatberry Dashboard, navigate to your instance URL and log in with your credentials.

### Account Verification

Upon first login, you may need to verify your email or phone number if 2FA is enabled.

---

## 2. Dashboard Overview

The main dashboard provides a high-level view of your current operations:

- **Notification Center**: View important system alerts and updates.
- **Quick Actions**: Shortcuts to common tasks like sending a message or creating a campaign.

---

## 3. WhatsApp Integration

### Setup

Navigate to **Settings > WhatsApp** to configure your connection.

- **Embedded Signup**: Use the "Setup WhatsApp" button to connect your Facebook/Meta account directly.
- **Manual Config**: Enter your App ID, Phone Number ID, and WABA ID manually if required.

### Business Profile

Under **Settings > WhatsApp**, you can manage your public profile:

- **Profile Picture**: Upload your business logo (min 192x192px).
- **Business Details**: profound address, email, description, and industry category.

### Template Management

Navigate to **Templates** to manage WhatsApp message templates.

- **Create**: Define new templates with headers (text/media), body text, footers, and buttons.
- **Status**: Monitor approval status (Pending, Approved, Rejected) from Meta.

---

## 4. Chat Management

The **Chats** section is the central hub for real-time communication.

- **Live Chat**: View and reply to incoming messages.
- **Media**: Send and receive images, documents, and audio.
- **Templates**: Send pre-approved templates to initiate conversations outside the 24-hour window.
- **Assignment**: Assign specific chats to team members for specialized support.
- **Notes**: Add internal notes to conversations for team collaboration.

---

## 5. Contacts & Groups

Manage your customer base in the **Contacts** section.

- **Contact List**: View, search, and filter contacts.
- **Import/Export**: Bulk import contacts via CSV or export your current list.
- **Groups**: Organize contacts into logical groups (e.g., "VIP", "New Leads") for targeted campaigns.
- **Custom Fields**: Define custom data fields to store specific information about your contacts.

---

## 6. Campaigns

Use **Campaigns** to send bulk messages to your audience.

- **Create Campaign**: Select a target contact group and a message template.
- **Scheduling**: Send immediately or schedule for a future date/time.
- **Media**: Attach media to your campaign messages.
- **Analytics**: Track delivery rates, read receipts, and failure reasons.

---

## 7. Automation

Automate responses to ensure 24/7 availability.

- **Auto-Replies**: Set up keywork-based automatic responses.
- **Canned Replies**: Create shortcuts for frequently used messages to speed up agent responses.
- **Flow Builder**: (If enabled) Design complex conversation flows to guide users through common queries.

---

## 8. Support Tickets

The **Tickets** system helps track and resolve customer issues.

- **Creation**: Create tickets for support requests
- **Management**: Assign priority (Low, Medium, High), change status (Open, Closed), and assign to agents.
- **Comments**: Add internal comments to tickets for team coordination.

---

## 9. Settings & Organization

### Team Management

- **Invite Members**: Add new users to your organization.
- **Roles**: Assign roles (Admin, Sentinel, Agent) to control access permissions.

### Organization Settings

- **Billing**: Manage your subscription plan and payment methods.
- **Timezone**: Set your organization's timezone for accurate scheduling.
- **API Access**: Generate access tokens for external integrations.

---

_Note: Features may vary based on your specific subscription plan and installed modules._
